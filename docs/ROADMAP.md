# Roadmap

Kinma Movers is built in three phases. Each phase is usable on its own, and each one makes the next easier.

## Phase 1 · Launch (in progress)

| Feature | Status |
|---|---|
| Customer quote and booking website | ✅ Built |
| Zone pricing from the Ikorodu hub, editable by the owner | ✅ Built |
| Driver application, approval, online switch, job acceptance, trip steps | ✅ Built |
| Owner dashboard: jobs, drivers, prices | ✅ Built |
| Smart driver matching with one-tap assign | ✅ Built |
| AI load assistant (describe your load, get the right size) | ✅ Built |
| Trip maps for customers and drivers | ✅ Built |
| SMS / WhatsApp alerts to customers and drivers | ⏳ Next |
| Customer trip tracking page (by booking number) | ⏳ Next |

## Phase 2 · Trust and money

- **Paystack payments:** card, transfer and USSD, with the driver's share paid out automatically.
- **Live GPS tracking:** the customer watches the truck move, like a ride-hailing app.
- **Photo proof:** the driver photographs the load at pickup and at delivery, and both photos are saved on the booking.
- **Ratings both ways:** customers rate drivers, and drivers rate customers (for example, a customer who wasn't ready at pickup).
- **Driver documents:** licence, vehicle papers and guarantor stored and checked in the dashboard.

## Phase 3 · Grow

- Android and iOS apps for customers and drivers.
- More vehicle sizes (tricycle cargo, 3-tonne trucks) with prices per vehicle.
- Business accounts for shops, furniture makers and estate agents, with monthly invoicing.

## What will make Kinma Movers different

These are the ideas that no mini-truck service in Lagos offers together today. They are ordered by how much they help customers and drivers, and how soon we can build them.

### 1. Book on WhatsApp, in English or Pidgin
Most Lagos customers already live on WhatsApp. An AI booking assistant on WhatsApp could take a message like *"I wan move 2 bedroom from Igbogbo go Ajah on Saturday"*. It would reply with the price, confirm the booking and send updates. No app download, and no website visit needed.

### 2. Snap your load, get your price
The customer takes two or three photos of what they're moving. AI identifies the items, estimates the load size, flags anything that won't fit a Suzuki Carry, and suggests helpers. Disputes on the day ("you didn't say there was a freezer") mostly disappear.

### 3. Return-load matching (no empty trips home)
Every trip out of Ikorodu ends with the truck driving home empty. Kinma Movers can offer discounted "going back to Ikorodu" slots on the same trip. That fills empty trucks, gives customers cheaper moves and earns drivers more. It's the single biggest efficiency gain for a hub-based business.

### 4. Shared loads for small items
Several customers with a few boxes each, going the same direction, share one truck and split the cost. This works like pooled rides, but for goods.

### 5. Fair, explainable driver matching
Matching already considers distance, whether the driver is online, their rating and how many trips they've had today, so work is shared fairly. Next it will learn from accept times and cancellations, and always show the owner *why* a driver was chosen.

### 6. AI driver sourcing
- Screen new driver applications automatically: check plate and licence formats and missing details, and flag anything for a phone call.
- Predict busy days (month-end, Saturdays, festive seasons) from booking history. The owner can then line up extra drivers from their network before demand arrives.
- Draft recruitment messages for WhatsApp groups and track which drivers came from which referral.

### 7. Protection for goods
Optional goods-in-transit cover at checkout, with pickup and delivery photos as evidence if a claim is ever made.

### 8. Scheduled and recurring moves
Shops that restock weekly, or students moving every semester, can book once and repeat.

### 9. Honest pricing, always shown first
The price is always shown before booking, the breakdown is always visible, and the driver can't change it on the day. This is already built and will remain a promise of the brand.
